History of the business

It all started back in high school, during holiday season. My friends and I in my neighborhood would wash dustbins on waste disposal day for a few neighbours in our street. It was a good way to keep ourselves busy. Then after high school while some of my frineds went to varsity, I went to the local collage were i still continued washing dustbins. Thanks to the collage and investment programmes, I managed to get funded to grow the business where now I have a new facility or container where i still continue to wash dustbins and cars.
