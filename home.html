Welcome to Xeno Wash Blybunk

Xeno Wash Blybunk is Blybunks local dustbin washing service and also car washing service. The dustbin cleaning service focuses on collecting customer's dustbins on waste disposal days where than we move waste into waste disposal bags and then clean the bins. We after wards provide a clean waste disposal bag with the clean dustbin and deliver it back to the customer's homes. While the car washing service allows customers to get their vehicles cleaned at our facility.
